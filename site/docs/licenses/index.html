Licenses

Openify is GPL-3.0-or-later. Fonts, icons, headphone data and Rust dependencies retain their own licenses.

Openify's license

Cargo.toml declares GPL-3.0-or-later: GNU GPL version 3 or any later version. The complete terms are in LICENSE.

Using Openify

  • You can run, study and modify Openify, and share copies under the GPL.
  • Private modifications do not require publication.
  • The license excludes warranty to the extent permitted by law.

Spotify's service has separate terms.

Forks and redistribution

  • Distributing binaries, including unmodified builds, requires providing Corresponding Source through a GPL-permitted method.
  • Preserve copyright and license notices. Modified versions must identify changes and their dates and carry the applicable GPL terms.
  • You cannot impose further restrictions on recipients' GPL rights.

Contributions use GPL-3.0-or-later. For new dependencies, the contributing guide requires GPL-3.0 compatibility and a cargo deny check licenses check.

OPRA headphone data

OPRA, started by Roon Labs and maintained with community contributors, licenses manufacturer, product and EQ data under CC BY-SA 4.0; its repository code is MIT.

Openify downloads the database on first profile search and caches it. Selected profiles credit their creators in Settings. Fitting a curve to the 10-band equalizer creates an adaptation. See assets/licenses/OPRA-NOTICE.txt.

Spotify

Openify is independent of Spotify AB. Spotify's name and logo are Spotify AB trademarks.

Playback requires Premium and uses unofficial interfaces; undocumented endpoints may change or stop working. Third-party clients may conflict with Spotify's terms. Openify caches audio for playback and has no music export feature.
