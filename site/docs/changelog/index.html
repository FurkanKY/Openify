Changelog

Download installers, portable builds and checksums from GitHub Releases.

0.1.4

14 November 2026.

Added

  • Smart lists: three self-updating lists in your library, built only from what you play in Openify on this computer. On repeat · 30 days, Liked, never played and Long time no play. They play and shuffle like any list, are never written to Spotify and can be hidden in Settings.

Changed

  • Redesigned now-playing pill: one centred axis with the play button in the middle, times on both sides of the controls, progress as a thin line along the bottom edge and quieter tool buttons.

0.1.3

13 November 2026.

Added

  • Mini player: a small always-on-top window with cover, title, controls, progress and like. Open it from the now-playing pill, the Ctrl+K palette or its shortcut; its position and open state are remembered.
  • Play counts: artist pages show monthly listeners, top listening cities and a play count on every popular song; album pages show a play count per song. They load in the background and are cached for a day.
  • Playlist editing: edit the description of your own playlists, turn collaborative on or off, and create, rename and delete folders and move playlists in and out of them. Every edit can be undone and waits safely while offline.

Changed

  • Song lists on album, artist and search pages have the same column headers as the library, and an album shows its artists on every row or on none.

0.1.2

13 November 2026.

Added

  • Light theme alongside Dark (default) and System. System follows Windows app mode, including the title bar.
  • Native ARM64 installer, Openify-win-arm64-Setup.exe, with a separate update channel.
  • winget manifests and a script to fill release hashes.
  • Optional Discord Rich Presence: song, artist, cover and progress. Hidden until a Discord application ID is configured at build time.
  • OPENIFY_TEST_THEME=light|dark for isolated visual tests.

Changed

  • Perceptual cover-colour blending, lyric fade-ins and sliding toggle switches.
  • Centred Settings and Tools columns in wide windows.

Fixed

  • Search shows a connection error after 10 seconds if Spotify is unreachable.

0.1.1

13 November 2026.

Changed

  • Wider, cover-tinted search box. Before typing: recent searches, repeat listening and genre shortcuts. Results: top result, songs, artists, albums and playlists, navigable with arrow keys. Commands appear for matching queries.
  • Wider seek bar on its own player row. Lists end above the floating player, with a fade behind it.

Fixed

  • Home, Explore and similar artists use the interface language (English or Turkish); Explore caches each language separately.
  • Home uses the computer's time zone instead of Europe/Istanbul.

Builds

  • GitHub Actions release builds with SHA-256 checksums in SHA256SUMS.txt.

0.1.0

12 November 2026. First public release.

Added

  • Windows client for Spotify Premium: Rust, gpui, librespot and local SQLite storage.
  • Browser sign-in; session stored in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Home, Explore, Library, artist, album and search pages; library folders, pins and sorting. English and Turkish interface.
  • Ctrl+K command palette, keyboard shortcuts, media keys, tray icon, Windows media overlay and optional global search hotkey.
  • Gapless playback, optional crossfade, normalization, up to 320 kbps and output-device recovery.
  • 10-band equalizer, presets, preamp and OPRA headphone profiles.
  • Duplicate/version finder, playlist merge/split, permanent sorting, JSON backup, local statistics, undo and queued offline edits.
  • Synced lyrics in side panel or full screen, LRCLIB fallback and optional translation.
  • Shuffle and queue rules: one version per song, skip explicit songs, block artists and hide songs. Radio from songs, artists, albums or playlists; private session.
  • Spotify Connect, on by default and switchable in Settings.
  • Track credits, similar artists and artist following. Experimental friend activity, off by default.
  • Installer with automatic updates, portable zip and rollback after two failed starts of a new version.

Limitations at release

  • No screen-reader support. Windows and dark theme only; light theme followed in 0.1.2.
  • Openify's own playback sends no listening reports to Spotify. Connect playback may be counted.
  • Friend activity and artist following had limited live-account testing.
