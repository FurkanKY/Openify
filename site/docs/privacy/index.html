Privacy and security

Openify stores settings, caches and listening history on your computer. Network requests support playback and the services listed below.

Servers and telemetry

Openify has no backend, analytics or telemetry. It connects to external services for music, metadata and updates.

Local storage

Default locations are under your Windows user profile:

DataLocationDetails
Library, lyrics, play history and hidden songs%LOCALAPPDATA%\Openify\library.dbSQLite; history and hidden songs stay local.
Cached pages%LOCALAPPDATA%\Openify\pages.dbArtist, album, Home and Explore data.
Covers and encrypted audio%LOCALAPPDATA%\Openify\covers, audioLimits: 200 MiB and 2 GiB.
Headphone profiles%LOCALAPPDATA%\Openify\eqOPRA database and metadata.
Logs and crash reports%LOCALAPPDATA%\Openify\openify.log, crash-<time>.txtFive crash reports retained; no automatic upload.
Settings and pending library edits%APPDATA%\Openify\settings.json, edits.dbEdits are saved before submission to Spotify.
Spotify sessionWindows Credential ManagerOpenify / spotify-session; no credential file.

Settings clears audio and cover caches. Deleting library.db also removes local history and hidden songs. Sign out to remove the saved session, or delete its Credential Manager entry.

Sign-in

Sign in on Spotify's page in your browser. A temporary listener at 127.0.0.1:8898/login receives the redirect; Openify never sees your password. The session is saved in Credential Manager.

The app uses Spotify's desktop client ID, a public source constant required by its internal services. Building Openify needs no API key.

External services

ServicePurpose and timing
SpotifySign-in, streaming, metadata, library edits, search, Home and Explore while online.
Image serversUncached covers and artist images, including Spotify and Deezer images.
LRCLIBLyrics fallback, including when Spotify has only unsynced lyrics. Requests include track and artist names, and may include album and duration.
DeezerSimilar artists; artist names are sent.
OPRAHeadphone database from opra.roonlabs.net, loaded when searching profiles.
GitHub ReleasesUpdate checks after startup, every six hours and on manual request; update downloads.
Spotify friend feedExperimental; off by default. Requests run when enabled and the panel is open.
Discord desktop appOptional Rich Presence through local IPC.

Friend activity, radio, track credits and lyrics translation use undocumented Spotify endpoints and may stop working.

Playback reporting

Openify's own playback sends no listening reports or scrobbles. Spotify's Recently played, Wrapped, play counts and friend activity may therefore omit these plays. Spotify may count playback transferred from another device through Connect. Openify computes its own recent plays and statistics locally.

Spotify Connect

Connect is on by default. Openify registers a persistent device ID with your account, named Openify (<computer name>). Rename it or turn Connect off in Settings; when off, no device is registered.

Private session

Enable a private session in the profile menu to stop local history and statistics recording for that session. An icon in the title bar marks it.

Discord Rich Presence

DISCORD_CLIENT_ID is empty in the source, so builds from this source have no Discord worker or settings section. Builds with an application ID expose an opt-in setting. When enabled, local named-pipe messages carry the title, artist, cover link and progress to Discord. Private sessions, pause, stop or disabling the setting clear activity.

Updates

Velopack downloads updates from GitHub Releases and applies them on the next launch. Set OPENIFY_UPDATE_URL to another feed URL or off to disable checks.

After two failed starts, rollback uses a locally saved working version, including offline. That failed version is blocked until a newer release. Update health state and the rollback package also occupy local cache storage.

The installer is unsigned and may trigger SmartScreen. Download from github.com/FurkanKY/Openify. To continue past the warning, choose More info → Run anyway.

Reporting a vulnerability

Use Security → Report a vulnerability for a private report. Include impact, Openify and Windows versions, and reproduction steps. Remove credentials and tokens from attachments.

Only the latest release receives security fixes. The policy targets acknowledgement within a few days; fixes are best-effort and reports stay private until a fixed release. Report Openify code and packaging problems here, upstream issues to their projects; dependency vulnerabilities affecting Openify are welcome here too.

Spotify's terms

Openify is independent of Spotify AB and requires Premium. It uses unofficial, reverse-engineered interfaces that may conflict with Spotify's terms. Spotify's name and logo belong to Spotify AB. Audio is cached for playback; the app has no music export feature.
